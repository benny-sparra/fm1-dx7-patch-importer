# M-VAVE SMK-37 Pro: FX run, 2026-11-03

Results of parts of `smk37-fx-run-sheet.md` on Ben's SMK-37 Pro, stock V015
(`SMK-37 Pro_015`), over USB to a Mac, reported by Ben the same day. Every message sent was a
standard Control Change or Note On/Off to `SMK-37 Pro-Private`. No SysEx was sent.

## Summary

- **The FX page's knobs send no MIDI.** K1–K5 sent nothing with Latch on or off, and with the
  page shown, held, or released.
- **The FX page is live only.** It is heard, but a preset change resets it to Cut Off 100 and the
  rest 0, and SAVE does not keep it.
- **The FM1's V15 effect controllers do nothing**, on channel 2 or channel 1.
- Still untried: V16's positions (CC 70–93, beyond the first run's 70 and 71) and General MIDI's
  CC 74, 71, 91, 93, and 94.

## Settings seen

GLOBE shows **Keys Channel-1** and **Pads Channel-10**. No FX or effect channel setting was
reported.

## 1. Listen to the knobs

A 10-minute capture on the `Private` and `Master` ports while K1–K5 were turned in every case the
sheet lists (Latch On; Latch Off with the page held; Latch Off with the page released). It saw
only the notes played and one burst of CC 67 (soft pedal) on channel 1 from a control Ben did not
identify. Nothing arrived when an FX knob turned.

So the release notes' "with Latch off, the knobs send standard MIDI messages" did not show on the
FX page on V015. Whether it holds for the Patch and Para pages was not tried.

## 2. The FX page from the panel

Each knob was heard changing the sound. After choosing another preset the page read Cut Off 100,
Distortion 0, Reverb 0, Delay 0 again, whatever the knobs had been set to, and SAVE did not keep
the values. The manual's "these parameters can also be saved to presets" does not hold on V015.

## 4. The FM1's V15 effect controllers

Under a held note on channel 1, each sent on channel 2 and then channel 1: CC 0, 1, 2 (Filter
switch, type, cutoff), 12, 13, 15 (Distortion switch, gain, level), 4, 7 (Reverb switch, mix),
and 8, 11 (Delay switch, mix). No change in the sound or on the FX page.

A clean before-and-after retest of Filter on (`CC 0 = 1`), low-pass (`CC 1 = 0`), and cutoff 0
(`CC 2 = 0`), on channel 1 and on channel 2: the sound was the same before and after.

## Not run

Steps 3 (nothing to send back), 5, 6, 7, and 8.

## Next candidates

None. A static read of V015's firmware the same day (`docs/fm1-research.md`, "What V015's
firmware shows") found that external MIDI reaches the sound only as DX7 dumps, DX7 parameter
changes, notes, aftertouch, pitch bend, and CC 1, 2, 4, and 64, on any channel. No Control Change
reaches the FX page, so steps 5–7 of the run sheet cannot find one on this release. They stay in
the sheet for a later firmware.

## What the results decide

- The SMK's profile keeps no `effectControllers`; PR 444's hidden effects panel stands.
- If a later step finds a controller, the effects are a live setting that a preset change
  resets, so they would not travel with a patch the way FM1 V15's do.
