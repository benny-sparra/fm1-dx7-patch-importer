# M-VAVE V16: follow-up run, 2026-10-25

Results of `stock-v16-follow-up-run-sheet.md` on the orange FM1, still on V16 (`FM-1_016`) since
2026-10-22, with the editor from the dev server. It covers what the run of 2026-10-23
(`stock-v16-2026-10-23.md`) left unchecked. Each step keeps the firmware's prediction (research
notes, "What the firmware shows") beside what the FM1 did. Photos are in `stock-v16-2026-10-25/`,
without their metadata.

## Summary

Confirmed on this FM1:

- **Receive patch…** took bank B's Export, and the editor sent nothing while it listened.
- Bank Export with no USB host does not show **Connect USB**, as the firmware reading had it. It
  starts sending, times out (**Receive Timeout**), refuses a second try at once (**USB Busy**),
  and recovers without a power cycle.
- A 32-voice bank sent by **Send to FM1** brings up **32 Voice Save To …** with A–D, as V15 did.
  Knob 1–4 saves to that bank at once, with no confirmation, and the save survives a power cycle.
  Turning SELECT left closes the prompt without writing anything. It does not time out.
- With two Voices On:
  - A Program Change reaches the Voice on its channel.
  - A single-voice dump reaches the Voice **selected on the FM1's screen**, whatever channel it
    arrives on.
  - Parameter changes, which the editor sends as device 1, reach the selected Voice **only while
    it listens on channel 1** (or All, as on 2026-10-23).
  - Notes reach the Voice on their channel.
- A short press of SAVE on a voice page offers Preset, as a hold does.

Found on the way:

- The preset list marks an unsaved edit with `*`, and shows a name changed by parameter changes
  at once. The header above it keeps the old name until the preset is selected again.
- Nothing came back from the FM1 apart from its identity answer and the exported bank.
- At 11:29:53 the editor sent one slot's Program Change and dump twice, 216 ms apart. Whether that
  was one click is not known; the editor's repeat check should stop a second, identical send.

Not checked: Undo after importing a received bank (section 1, step 4).

## 2. Bank Export without USB

Firmware: Export checks for a USB host first and shows **Connect USB** without one, sending
nothing.

The cable was unplugged at the Mac's end each time, so the FM1 had neither host nor power: its
battery icon is white in each photo until the cable went back in, and green after.

1. On battery, Export of bank A shows **"Sending Bank A / 0% / SELECT Left: Cancel"**
   ([photo](stock-v16-2026-10-25/export-on-battery-sending.jpg)). It stays at 0% and ends with
   **"Bank A / Receive Timeout / SELECT Left: Back"**
   ([photo](stock-v16-2026-10-25/export-on-battery-receive-timeout.jpg)).
2. Export again at once: **"Bank A / USB Busy / SELECT Left: Back"**
   ([photo](stock-v16-2026-10-25/export-on-battery-usb-busy.jpg)). The failed transfer still
   counts as running.
3. Plugging the cable into the Mac during a send: **"Bank A / USB Disconnected / SELECT Left:
   Back"** ([photo](stock-v16-2026-10-25/export-usb-plugged-in-mid-send.jpg)).
4. Then Export with **Receive patch…** listening worked at once, with no power cycle.

**Result: differs from the firmware reading.** **Connect USB** never showed: without a host the
FM1 starts the transfer and reports it failing. Nothing is harmed, since it recovers by itself and
the editor sends nothing; instructions for Bank Export should say to connect USB and open the
receive dialog before starting Export.

## 3. Sending a bank

Firmware: after a 32-voice bank arrives, the FM1 waits for a destination bank and writes nothing
until one is chosen.

1. The editor bank "billy" (BRASSBEN, BRASS 2, BRASS 3, STRINGS 1, …) was sent with **Send to
   FM1**, twice: once to A and once to B.
2. The FM1 shows **32 Voice Save To …** above four round buttons **A B C D**, over the page it was
   on (OSC here) ([photo](stock-v16-2026-10-25/bank-prompt-32-voice-save-to.jpg)). It looks as it
   did on V15.
3. The editor's instructions: Knob 1–4 choose A–D, which holds. Its "saves … automatically after a
   brief delay" does not: the FM1 saved as soon as the knob was turned. Nothing said how to
   cancel.
4. Bank A on the first send, then bank B: 001 and 033 read BRASSBEN, and banks A and B hold "billy". No
   confirmation. Both were still there after a power cycle.
5. Cancel: with the prompt up, turning **SELECT left** closes it. Left alone, it does not time
   out. 001 and 033 still read BRASSBEN afterwards, so nothing was written.
6. `V16 bank B` was sent back to bank B (Knob 2), and 033 read GUITAR 1 again. Bank A keeps
   "billy".

**Result: as the firmware showed.** The bank destination instructions now say that the FM1 saves
as soon as a bank is chosen, and that SELECT left cancels (PR #360).

## 4. Where a patch lands with two Voices

Set up: Voice 1 on channel 1 and Voice 2 on channel 3, both On; Voices 3–8 Off. Voice 1 played
033 (GUITAR 1). FM1 bank A held "billy", the same patches as the
editor's bank A, so names were changed in the editor to see where each message landed.

Firmware: a Program Change loads its program into every Voice that is On on its channel or on All.
A dump and the parameter changes edit the preset on the Voice selected on the FM1's screen,
whatever channel they arrive on.

What the editor sent, from its MIDI log (dumps shortened; each is `F0 43 02 00 01 1B`, 155 bytes,
a checksum, and `F7`, sent on device 3 for channel 3):

```text

11:21:48.970  F0 43 02 00 01 1B …   dump of BRASS   2
11:21:57.600  C2 02                 Program 3 on channel 3     (A03 BRASS 3)
11:21:57.600  F0 43 02 00 01 1B …   dump of BRASS   3
11:22:00.133  C2 01                 Program 2 on channel 3     (A02 again)
11:22:00.133  F0 43 02 00 01 1B …   dump of BRASS   2
11:22:38.450  C2 06                 Program 7 on channel 3     (A07 ORCHESTRA)
11:22:38.450  F0 43 02 00 01 1B …   dump of ORCHESTRA
11:27:08.683  C2 06                 editor opened on A07
11:27:09.031  F0 43 02 00 01 1B …   dump of ORCHESTRA
11:27:14.999  F0 43 10 01 15 20 F7  parameter 149 = ' '        renamed ORCH TEST,
…             F0 43 10 01 16–19 …   parameters 150–153         Voice 1 selected
11:29:42.478  F0 43 10 01 14 20 F7  parameter 148 = ' '        renamed ORC TEST2,
…             F0 43 10 01 15–19 …   parameters 149–153         Voice 2 selected
11:29:53.740  C2 06                 slot A07 clicked,          Voice 2 selected
11:29:53.741  F0 43 02 00 01 1B …   dump of ORC TEST2
11:29:53.956  C2 06                 the same again, 216 ms later
11:29:54.034  F0 43 02 00 01 1B …   dump of ORC TEST2
```

1. **Voice 1 selected, editor note channel 3, slot click.** The Program Change moved **Voice 2**,
   which ended on 007 ORCHESTRA; Voice 1 stayed on 033. The dumps reached **Voice 1**: its 033 read
   BRASS 2, then ORCHESTRA. As the firmware showed: the click split, the program on one Voice and
   the sound on the other.
2. **Voice 1 selected, the editor open, a rename.** The five parameter changes made Voice 1's 033
   **ORCH TEST \*** ([photo](stock-v16-2026-10-25/voice1-live-rename.jpg)). That needs the
   ORCHESTRA dump to have reached it first, or it would read BRAS TEST. Voice 2's 007 read
   ORCHESTRA, unedited.
3. **Voice 2 selected (channel 3), a rename.** Neither Voice changed: Voice 2's 007 kept its name
   after scrolling away and back, and Voice 1's 033 stayed ORCH TEST. A slot click then put the
   dump, **ORC TEST2**, on **Voice 2**'s 007. So the dump follows the selected Voice. Renaming to
   ORC TEST3 the same way changed nothing until the patch was saved and sent whole.
4. **Voice 2 selected and moved to channel 1, a rename** to ORC TEST6: Voice 2's 007 read **ORC
   TEST6 \*** at once, and kept it after scrolling
   ([photo](stock-v16-2026-10-25/voice2-on-channel-1-live-rename.jpg)). The header above the
   list still read ORC TEST3 until 007 was selected again
   ([photo](stock-v16-2026-10-25/header-after-reselect.jpg)). So parameter changes, which the
   editor sends as device 1, reach the selected Voice only while it listens on channel 1. With
   Voice 1 on All on 2026-10-23, they reached it too.
5. **On-screen keyboard**, Voice 1 on 035 BASS 1 and Voice 2 back on channel 3: note channel 1
   played the bass, note channel 3 ORC TEST6. Notes follow the channel.
6. **A short press of SAVE** on a voice page (OSC) offers Knob1 ALL, Knob2 Globe, Knob3
   **Preset**, Knob4 Pattern (greyed), and SAVE to cancel, as a hold does
   ([photo](stock-v16-2026-10-25/save-short-press-from-osc.jpg)). Backed out without saving.

| Message                                  | Addressed by              | Reached                                               |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Program Change                           | MIDI channel              | Every Voice On on that channel                        |
| Single-voice dump                        | Device (the note channel) | The Voice selected on the FM1, whatever the device    |
| Parameter change                         | Device 1 only             | The selected Voice, only while it listens on 1 or All |
| Note On and Off (the on-screen keyboard) | MIDI channel              | The Voice on that channel                             |

## What the results decide

- **Bank send.** The destination instructions keep their knob steps, say the FM1 saves at once,
  and say SELECT left cancels (PR #360).
- **Which Voice the editor edits.** On V16 a slot click, the editor's opening dump, and **Send**
  reach the Voice selected on the FM1, while the Program Change goes to the Voice on the note
  channel, and live edits reach only a selected Voice on channel 1 or All. The editor gets what
  it expects only when the Voice on its note channel is the one selected on the FM1 and listens
  on channel 1 or All, as Voice 1 does on a stock FM1. The firmware setting or the editor's
  notice should say so on V16; the backlog's V16 item, step 3, takes it from here.
- **Bank Export.** Without a USB host it fails as Receive Timeout and recovers; a receive wizard
  should say to connect USB and start listening before Export, and that a timeout means trying
  again.
- **The double send** at 11:29:53 needs a look at the editor's repeat check before anything else
  is concluded from it.
