# M-VAVE SMK-37 Pro: hardware run, 2026-11-03

Results of `smk37-run-sheet.md` on Ben's SMK-37 Pro, on the stock firmware it shipped with, over
USB to a Mac. Every message was sent once from `smk37-send.swift` beside this file (a CoreMIDI
tool that sends only standard channel messages and Yamaha `F0 43` SysEx to the `SMK-37 Pro-Private`
port), apart from the identity query, sent by a one-off script with the editor's exact bytes. The
editor itself was not used, so each step sent exactly one kind of message.

## Summary

- **Identity: `SMK-37 Pro_015`, stock V015.** The reply is the FM1's format byte for byte: 41
  bytes, the block `00 59 11`, a declared length of 27, the name zero-padded. The editor's
  `parseFm1IdentityReply` reads it; `classifyFm1Firmware` calls it unidentified.
- **Ports.** USB device `SMK-37 Pro Midi`, VID:PID **4353:CF4D** (the port kit had B64B for
  V016), `bcdDevice` 0x0100, no product string. CoreMIDI lists sources and destinations
  `SMK-37 Pro-Private`, `SMK-37 Pro-Master`, and `Port 3`. Only `Private` answered the identity
  query; everything below went to it. With only the SMK connected, `resolveMidiPortSelection`
  picks `Private` as the first real port. Audio arrives as `SMK-37 Pro Audio`, 2 channels in and
  out at 44.1 kHz.
- **Channels.** Notes play on all 16 channels.
- **Program Change is ignored**: programs 0, 1, 31, 32, and 127 on channel 1 left the preset and
  sound unchanged.
- **DX7 parameter changes reach the voice as an unsaved edit**: Algorithm 32 was heard at once,
  and selecting another preset and back restored the stored sound.
- **A DX7 single-voice dump is saved over the selected preset.** The sound changed at once, was
  still there after selecting another preset and back, and after a power cycle. This is
  FM-1+VA's behaviour, not the stock FM1's edit buffer. The test preset is now `SMK TEST`.
- **No bank export** in the keyboard's menus, so no backup could be taken and step 8 (sending a
  bank) was skipped, as the sheet requires after this result.
- **Effect controllers: nothing heard** from CC 0–1 or CC 70–71 on channel 2 under a held note.
- Over the whole run the SMK sent nothing over MIDI but the identity reply.

## 2. Identity

Sent `F0 00 32 45 00 00 00 40 7F F7` (`fm1IdentityQuery`) to `Private`, then `Master`. `Private`
answered at once; `Master` did not.

```text
F0 00 32 45 58 01 00 40 29 4D 16 35 19 73 06 08 28 72 5E 7D 02 13 26 0D 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
00 00 00 00 00 00 60 0D F7
```

Unpacked block: `00 59 11 1B 00 00` then `SMK-37 Pro_015` and zeros. On the device's own screen
013 shows as 1.10 (Elektronauts); V015's screen version was not read.

## 3. What it plays

Middle C on each channel 1–16 in turn: all 16 sounded.

## 4. Program Change

| Sent    | Preset shown | Sound changed? |
| ------- | ------------ | -------------- |
| `C0 00` | unchanged    | no             |
| `C0 01` | unchanged    | no             |
| `C0 1F` | unchanged    | no             |
| `C0 20` | unchanged    | no             |
| `C0 7F` | unchanged    | no             |

Not tried: Bank Select before the Program Change, other channels, and any receive setting in the
keyboard's menus.

## 5. DX7 parameter change

`F0 43 10 01 06 1F F7` (Algorithm 32, as `makeFm1ParameterPayload` builds it) on the current
preset: heard at once. Selecting another preset and back: the stored sound returned.

## 6. DX7 single-voice dump

A standard 163-byte dump on channel 1 (`F0 43 00 00 01 1B`, INIT VOICE's values named
`SMK TEST`): the sound became the sine at once; still the sine after selecting another preset
and back; still the sine after a power cycle without saving. The dump writes the selected stored
preset.

## 7. Bank export

The keyboard's menus have no export. No backup of its presets exists.

## 8. Sending a bank

Skipped: step 6 showed a dump writes storage, so a bank dump would very likely replace a stored
bank, with no backup to restore it.

## 9. Effect controllers

Under a held middle C on channel 1, on channel 2: `B1 00 7F`, `B1 01 00`, `B1 01 6B`, `B1 00 00`,
then the same at CC 70 and 71. Nothing heard either time.

Not tried: other channels (the SMK may have its own FX channel setting), the rest of each range,
and CC 1, 7, and 11, which V16 takes as modulation and volume.

## What the results decide

- The SMK-37 can be identified from the identity's model, `SMK-37 Pro`, before the underscore:
  a family of its own in `classifyFm1Firmware` and `familyProfiles`.
- Its profile has `parameterChanges` only: no `singleVoiceDumps`, no `programChanges`, and no
  `effectControllers` until a channel that moves its effects is found. The editor already sends
  it parameter changes, as an unidentified firmware.
- **Today's editor is unsafe in one place:** **Send to FM1** sends an unidentified firmware a
  32-voice bank dump, which on this keyboard very likely replaces a stored bank. Identifying the
  SMK must stop that first.
- A slot click sends a Program Change it ignores, then the voice as parameter changes, so playing
  a patch works; the bank instructions and badge need SMK wording.
- `resolveMidiPortSelection` should prefer a port named `SMK`, and the `Private` port where the
  SMK lists several.
- Still open: the Elite's and MKE-P37's identities, the V016 firmware, an FX channel, Bank
  Select, and whether a dump goes to the preset on screen or one chosen otherwise.
